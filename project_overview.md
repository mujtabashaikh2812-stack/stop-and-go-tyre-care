# Project Overview - STOP & GO Total Tyre Care Centre

## System Purpose
An enterprise-grade, mobile-responsive Garage Management & Billing Application designed specifically for **STOP & GO Total Tyre Care Centre**. It digitizes traditional paper job cards, automates billing calculations, maintains customer visit history, tracks consumables inventory, and provides real-time financial analytics.

## Core Application Modules

### 1. 📋 New Job Card & Dynamic Billing (Paper Slip Digitization)
Digitizes all 9 service items from the physical receipt slip:
1. **Wheel Alignment**
2. **Wheel Balancing** (2 Tyre / 4 Tyre selection)
3. **Wheel Weight** (Sticker / Brass selector + Gram input)
4. **Tyre Fitting & Valves** (Fitting Qty + New Valve Qty & Rate)
5. **Tyre Rotation**
6. **Head Light Buffing & Cleaning**
7. **Air Filling** (Nitrogen Air / Normal Air toggle)
8. **Tubeless Puncher Repair** (Puncture Qty)
9. **Camber Setting** (Front R/L & Rear R/L toggles)

### 2. 🔍 Customer Lookup & History CRM
- **Auto-Fetching**: Enter customer phone number or vehicle registration to retrieve past visits instantly.
- **Service History**: View complete history of alignment, balancing, and parts replaced.
- **1-Tap WhatsApp Service Reminder**: Send 5,000 km alignment reminders directly to customer's WhatsApp.

### 3. 📊 Business Analytics Dashboard
- Live tracking of Daily & Monthly Revenue.
- Cash vs. UPI / QR Code Payment Breakdown.
- Vehicle volume counter and Top Performed Services leaderboard.

### 4. 📦 Inventory & Consumables Management
- Stock tracking for Sticker Weights (grams), Brass Weights (grams), Valves (pcs), Nitrogen Pressure.
- Automatic inventory deduction on bill generation + low stock warnings.

### 5. 🧾 Digital Invoicing & Thermal Printing
- Printable 80mm thermal receipt generator.
- 1-click WhatsApp bill summary launcher with deep link message.
